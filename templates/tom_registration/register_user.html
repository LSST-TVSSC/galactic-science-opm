Data protection and privacy (GDPR)

This service is operated as part of the LSST TVSSC Galactic Science Observing Program Manager (OPM). By registering you consent to the processing of your personal data as described below.

What data we store
  • Your username
  • Your name
  • Your email address
How we use your data
  • To manage your access to the OPM and associated tools
  • To contact you with project-related information (e.g. system changes, observing workflows)
  • To derive aggregated, anonymised statistics about TVS membership and OPM usage
How long we keep your data
  • Your account data is kept for as long as you remain an active OPM user and at most until the end of the Rubin Observatory LSST survey, unless you withdraw your consent earlier by deleting your account.
Sharing of data
  • Your information is stored in the TVS OPM database and is not shared with third parties outside the TVS Science Collaboration, except where required by law.
Your rights
  • You can view and update your data via your profile page after logging in.
  • You can delete your account at any time using the Delete account option (see user menu), which removes your personal data in the OPM.
  • You may contact the OPM administrators if you have questions about how your data is used.

This notice is intended to help align the OPM with GDPR requirements, but does not replace institutional legal advice.
